---

IPCE is a lightweight agent loop designed for internal automation and structured experimentation at ECPI.

It integrates an OpenAI-compatible language model with controlled tool execution, enabling multi-step reasoning and task-oriented workflows from the command line.

IPCE is _not_ positioned as a general chatbot.  
> It is an evolving automation core.

---

## 🛠 Purpose

IPCE serves as a foundation for:

- Academic tooling  
- Curriculum and code assistance  
- Internal workflow automation  
- Exploration of agent loop architectures  

The system is intentionally minimal and modular, allowing careful expansion without obscuring its reasoning structure.

---

## 🧬 Origins

IPCE began as an educational scaffold derived from an LLM agent-building exercise using [codecrafters.io](https://codecrafters.io/challenges/claude-code).  
> That foundation now serves as the basis for a research-oriented internal tool.
Its development reflects both personal exploration and a commitment to supporting the ECPI academic community.

---

## 🧭 Philosophy

IPCE is built on the belief that agent systems should be:

- Understandable before they are powerful  
- Observable before they are autonomous  
- Intentional before they are optimized  

The goal is not abstraction for its own sake, but clarity in how reasoning, tools, and control interact.

IPCE grows slowly, deliberately, and with structure.

---

## 🗺 Roadmap

IPCE is under steady development with focus on the following areas:

- Expanded internal tool registry  
- Structured logging and observability  
- Loop safety and execution controls  
- Workflow templates for academic use  
- Evaluation and refinement of reasoning cycles  

Development is iterative and research-driven.
